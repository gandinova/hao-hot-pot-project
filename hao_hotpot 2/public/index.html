About HAO

Where Tradition Meets Smart Hospitality

HAO HOT POT was born from a vision: to bring the rich culinary heritage of Chinese hot pot to Valencia while reimagining the dining experience through Smart Restaurant technology. Every element — from our QR-based digital ecosystem to our data-aware reservation system — reflects the principles of Smart Campus innovation studied in modern Travel Management.

Our name, "HAO" (好), means "good" in Chinese — a simple promise that guides everything we do. We source the finest ingredients, train our La Mian masters for months, and design every space to transport our guests into a world where dining is theatre.

This project serves as a practical exploration of how digital tools can enhance cultural hospitality experiences — a core research area within the Travel Management programme's focus on smart tourism and sustainable cultural integration.

Core Value

Immersive Experience

Inspired by Haidilao's legendary waiting-area experience, HAO HOT POT transforms every visit into a full-sensory journey. Before you even sit down, you're welcomed into a space designed to transport you — curated lighting that shifts with the time of day, ambient soundscapes blending traditional Chinese instruments with modern chill beats, and the aromatic embrace of 12-hour-simmered broths.

Real-world examples from Haidilao: The chain is famous for offering complimentary nail art, shoe shining, and board games while guests wait for their table. Birthday celebrations include a full noodle-dance performance and a complimentary dessert with a candle. Private dining rooms feature smart glass that switches between transparent and frosted at the touch of a button. Our version brings this same theatrical energy to Valencia.

Every detail — from the copper pot designs to the staff's training in performance service — is calibrated to make you feel like the protagonist of your own dining story.

Academic Note: Immersive dining experiences are a key case study in experiential travel theory within the Travel Management curriculum, demonstrating how multi-sensory engagement increases perceived value and guest retention.

Signature Performance

La Mian Show

The art of La Mian (拉面) — hand-pulled noodles — is one of China's oldest culinary traditions, dating back over 400 years to the Lanzhou region. At HAO HOT POT, this ancient craft becomes live theatre.

What you'll see: A trained La Mian master wheels a cart to your table carrying a block of rested dough. In one continuous fluid motion, they stretch, fold, twist, and pull — transforming that single block into dozens of perfectly uniform strands in under 60 seconds.

Haidilao's influence: Haidilao made the noodle dance famous globally. Their performers often add dramatic flourishes — spinning the noodles like a lasso, stretching them to arm's length, even performing blindfolded. We bring that same showmanship to Valencia.

Academic Note: The La Mian Show represents cultural heritage presentation within the Smart Restaurant framework — showcasing how traditional culinary techniques can be preserved and elevated through theatrical hospitality.

Core Value

Premium Service

Haidilao has been called "the Disneyland of hot pot" for a reason. Their service philosophy is legendary: anticipate every need before the guest articulates it. At HAO HOT POT, we've studied and adapted this philosophy for the Valencia market.

  • •Personal Host: Every table is assigned a dedicated host who introduces the menu, guides broth selection, and checks in at precisely the right moments.
  • •Thoughtful Amenities: Phone charging stations, hair ties, glasses cleaning cloths, aprons, and protective bags for your belongings — all complimentary.
  • •Celebration Service: Custom desserts, Polaroid photos, and staff performances for special occasions.
  • •Digital Profile: Our Smart Reservation system remembers your past orders, preferred broths, and allergies.

Academic Note: This service model aligns with Smart Hospitality research in Travel Management, where technology-enabled personalisation creates competitive advantage in the experiential economy.
